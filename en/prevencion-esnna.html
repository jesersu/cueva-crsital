Policy for the Prevention of the Sexual Exploitation of Girls, Boys and Adolescents (ESNNA)

Courtesy translation. In case of any discrepancy, the Spanish version prevails. Read the Spanish original.

Protégeme · Turismo Responsable (Protect me · Responsible Tourism) – to prevent the sexual exploitation of girls, boys and adolescents.

The agency's commitment

IN THIS AGENCY WE DO NOT PROMOTE OR PERMIT THE SEXUAL EXPLOITATION OF GIRLS, BOYS AND ADOLESCENTS, NOR ANY OTHER CRIMINAL OFFENCE OF WHICH WE BECOME AWARE IN THE COURSE OF OUR ACTIVITY, IN ACCORDANCE WITH LAW No. 29408.

In this travel agency, we do not promote or permit the sexual exploitation of girls, children and teenagers, nor any other crime of which we take knowledge in development of our activity, according to Law Nº 29408.

Legal framework

The legal provisions that impose criminal penalties for conduct linked to ESNNA include:

  • Law No. 29408 “General Tourism Law” (Ley General de Turismo) and its Regulations, approved by Supreme Decree No. 003-2010-MINCETUR.
  • Law No. 30963 “Law amending the Criminal Code regarding the penalties for the offence of sexual exploitation in its various forms and related offences, to protect with special emphasis girls, boys, adolescents and women”.
  • Law No. 30802 “Law establishing conditions for the admission of children and adolescents to lodging establishments in order to guarantee their protection and integrity”.
  • Article 153-B of the Criminal Code: Sexual exploitation.
  • Article 181-A of the Criminal Code: Commercial sexual exploitation of children and adolescents in the field of tourism.

Admission of minors to lodging establishments

IN THIS ESTABLISHMENT WE DO NOT ADMIT PERSONS UNDER 18 YEARS OF AGE WITHOUT THE COMPANY OF THEIR PARENTS, GUARDIAN OR RESPONSIBLE PERSON, DULY ACCREDITED, IN ACCORDANCE WITH LAW No. 30802.*

In this place, we do not admit under 18 years old without their parents, guardian or responsible person, properly accredited, according to Law Nº 30802.*

(*) This requirement does not apply to persons over sixteen (16) years of age who have acquired capacity through marriage by official title in accordance with the provisions of Article 46 of the Civil Code, who must prove that legal status with the corresponding public instruments.

(*) Exceptions to this requirement are persons of legal age (16) who acquired by marriage or official title in accordance with the article 46 of the Civil Code, who are obliged to prove that legal situation with the corresponding public instruments.

If you know of any case of sexual exploitation of persons under 18, do not hesitate to report it!

Your report can be anonymous if you prefer.
