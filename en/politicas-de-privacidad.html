Privacy and Personal Data Protection Policy

Courtesy translation. In case of any discrepancy, the Spanish version prevails. Read the Spanish original.

1. Data controller

CORPORACIÓN TULUMAYO MYSTIC S.A.C., RUC (tax ID) No. 20616494750, owner of LOS CRISTALES TRAVEL AGENCY, is responsible for the processing of the personal data collected through its digital channels and during the provision of tourism services.

Processing will be carried out in accordance with Law No. 29733, the Personal Data Protection Law (Ley de Protección de Datos Personales), its Regulations in force and other applicable provisions.

2. Data that may be collected

Depending on the nature of the service, LOS CRISTALES TRAVEL AGENCY may collect first names and surnames, DNI (national identity document) or passport, nationality, date of birth or age where necessary, telephone, email address, information needed for bookings, admissions or transport, and other data indispensable for performing the contracted service.

LOS CRISTALES TRAVEL AGENCY will avoid collecting information that is not necessary for the stated purpose.

3. Purposes

Personal data may be used to:

  • handle enquiries and requests;
  • prepare quotations;
  • manage and confirm bookings;
  • perform the contracted services;
  • coordinate with hotels, carriers, guides, operators and other providers;
  • issue receipts;
  • handle grievances or complaints;
  • communicate with the customer about their service;
  • comply with legal obligations.

Data will not be used for different advertising purposes without the corresponding consent where it is required.

4. Consent

Where the regulations require consent, it must be free, prior, express, unequivocal and informed. Acceptance of the Terms and Conditions does not automatically imply consent for purposes other than those necessary to manage and perform the tourism service.

5. Disclosure to third parties

LOS CRISTALES TRAVEL AGENCY may disclose only the strictly necessary data to providers involved in performing the service, such as accommodation, carriers, guides, local operators, managers of attractions and other providers needed to fulfil the contract.

Data may also be disclosed where there is a legal obligation or a valid request from a competent authority.

6. Security and confidentiality

LOS CRISTALES TRAVEL AGENCY will adopt reasonable security, confidentiality and control measures to prevent loss, unauthorised access, alteration, improper processing or disclosure of personal data.

Staff and providers with access to that information must use it only for the corresponding purpose.

7. Rights of the data subject

The data subject may exercise the rights recognised by the regulations in force, including access, rectification, updating, cancellation, objection and any others that apply.

8. Photographs, videos and promotional material

Contracting a tourism service does not automatically authorise LOS CRISTALES TRAVEL AGENCY to use identifiable photographs or videos of the passenger for advertising.

Where such images are used for promotional purposes, the corresponding prior consent will be requested, except where permitted by law.

9. Data of girls, boys and adolescents

The processing of personal data of girls, boys and adolescents will respect the special protection rules provided by the legislation in force and, where applicable, will involve the father, mother, guardian or legal representative.

10. Updating this policy

LOS CRISTALES TRAVEL AGENCY may update this policy to adapt it to regulatory, technological or operational changes. Amendments will not affect acquired rights nor allow data to be used retroactively for different purposes without the corresponding legal basis.
