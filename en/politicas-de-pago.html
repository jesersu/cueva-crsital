Payment, Cancellation and Refund Policies

Courtesy translation. In case of any discrepancy, the Spanish version prevails. Read the Spanish original.

1. Payment methods

The authorised payment methods will be communicated to the customer through the official digital channels of LOS CRISTALES TRAVEL AGENCY before any payment is made. The customer must first verify the identity of the account holder, the account number or digital wallet and the other details provided by LOS CRISTALES TRAVEL AGENCY.

LOS CRISTALES TRAVEL AGENCY may enable bank transfers, digital wallets or other payment methods, which will be stated clearly and verifiably before the contract is confirmed.

2. Booking and deposit

As a general rule, confirmation may require a deposit of 30 % of the total price of the package per person, unless a quotation previously sets another percentage because of specific supplier conditions.

The deposit forms part of the total contracted price and allows the corresponding arrangements, reservations and provisions to begin.

3. Outstanding balance

The deadline for paying the balance will be communicated before the booking is confirmed. Failure to pay will not automatically result in the total loss of what has been paid. LOS CRISTALES TRAVEL AGENCY will determine the costs actually committed and, where applicable, the recoverable balance.

4. Cancellation requested by the customer

Any cancellation must be communicated through a channel that allows its date to be proven. As a general guide, the following administrative deductions may apply to the total agreed price per person:

  • more than seven (7) calendar days before the start: up to 10 %;
  • between four (4) and seven (7) calendar days: up to 20 %;
  • within the seventy-two (72) hours before the start: up to 30 %.

The deduction must be proportionate to the administrative, commercial and operational actions actually carried out.

5. Non-recoverable supplier costs

In addition, amounts actually committed and non-recoverable may be deducted for accommodation, meals, transport, entrance tickets, flights where applicable, guides, operators, tourist activities, reservations and other services needed to fulfil the package.

To apply such deductions:

a) the condition must have been communicated to the customer beforehand;

b) the expense must be directly related to the customer's booking; and

c) LOS CRISTALES TRAVEL AGENCY must be able to support it with evidence.

No double deduction will be made for the same item.

6. Cancellation for a justified reason

Where the customer reports and proves a serious or reasonably justified situation, such as illness or accident, the death of an immediate family member, a family emergency, a proven work impediment or another similar circumstance, LOS CRISTALES TRAVEL AGENCY will first seek to reschedule.

Where a refund applies, the recoverable balance will be recognised after deducting only the applicable administrative costs and the third-party services that are actually non-recoverable.

7. Cancellation for the customer's convenience

Where the cancellation is due to a change of plans, personal convenience or a decision to hire another provider, the condition applicable at the time the cancellation is communicated will apply. Contracting with another provider does not in itself constitute an additional penalty.

8. No-show

The passenger's failure to attend without prior notice will be treated as a no-show. The services and costs actually committed and non-recoverable may be deducted. If there is a recoverable balance, it will be recognised in favour of the customer.

9. Withdrawal during the service

Where the customer decides to withdraw voluntarily after the service has started, no refund will be due for the benefits already used; the contracted benefits that are actually non-recoverable may be deducted; and any recoverable balance will be determined and communicated to the customer.

10. Cancellations due to external causes

Where the service cannot be performed because of weather conditions, closure of roads or attractions, orders of the authorities, safety risks, unforeseeable events, force majeure or other objective causes not attributable to LOS CRISTALES TRAVEL AGENCY, the agency will seek, as the case may be, rescheduling, substitution with an equivalent service or a refund of the amounts actually recoverable.

The loss of 100 % of the amount paid will not apply automatically.

11. Cancellation attributable to LOS CRISTALES TRAVEL AGENCY

Where the service cannot be provided for a reason directly attributable to LOS CRISTALES TRAVEL AGENCY, no administrative penalty will be applied to the consumer. The appropriate solution, rescheduling or refund will be provided for the services not performed, without prejudice to the rights recognised by applicable law.

12. Refund

Where a refund applies, LOS CRISTALES TRAVEL AGENCY will give the customer the details of the calculation:

  • Total contracted price: S/ ....................
  • Amount paid: S/ ....................
  • Administrative deduction: S/ ....................
  • Supported non-recoverable costs: S/ ....................
  • Final amount to be refunded: S/ ....................
  • Refund method: ........................................
  • Refund period: ........................................

The refund will be made by the method agreed with the customer, within the period previously communicated.
