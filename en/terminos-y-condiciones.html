Before receiving a payment or confirming a booking, LOS CRISTALES TRAVEL AGENCY will inform the customer, clearly and verifiably, of at least the following:

A quotation will be valid for the period expressly stated. Until confirmation and payment, rates may vary due to availability or changes in suppliers' prices.

The booking will be confirmed when LOS CRISTALES TRAVEL AGENCY confirms availability and the customer pays the agreed deposit or payment. The balance will be paid within the period stated when the contract is made.

Once the contract is confirmed, LOS CRISTALES TRAVEL AGENCY may make the reservations, arrangements and payments needed to secure accommodation, meals, transport, entrance tickets, guides or other authorised providers, tourist activities, local operators and any other services included in the package.

Where any of these services is non-refundable, partially refundable or subject to a penalty, this must be communicated to the customer. Any costs later deducted on this basis must be directly related to the customer's booking and be capable of being supported with evidence.

Prices may be expressed in soles or, where applicable, in US dollars. When the contract is made in a foreign currency and the customer chooses to pay in soles, LOS CRISTALES TRAVEL AGENCY must first state the exchange rate that will apply to the transaction.

Once the service is confirmed, the agreed price will be honoured, except for additional services requested by the customer or changes expressly accepted.

LOS CRISTALES TRAVEL AGENCY may reasonably modify schedules, routes, itineraries or suppliers where objective circumstances exist, such as weather conditions, closure of roads or attractions, orders of the authorities, safety risks, operational restrictions, unforeseeable events or force majeure.

8. Punctuality and pick-up

The passenger must be at the place and time previously communicated. LOS CRISTALES TRAVEL AGENCY will state the applicable waiting time when the service includes transport or a scheduled departure.

Where the passenger cannot take part because of a delay attributable to the passenger, the no-show conditions and the costs actually committed will apply as appropriate.

9. Safety and conduct

The passenger must follow the safety instructions given for each activity. Where a person is in a condition that poses a risk to themselves or to others, including obvious signs of intoxication by alcohol or other substances that affect their ability to take part safely, LOS CRISTALES TRAVEL AGENCY may prevent or suspend their participation.

The financial consequence will be determined according to the services already performed or committed, avoiding the automatic loss of the entire payment. For nature or adventure activities, LOS CRISTALES TRAVEL AGENCY must state in advance the applicable physical, technical and safety conditions.

10. Accommodation and third-party services

Where the package includes accommodation or other third-party services, these will be subject to availability and to the conditions previously communicated. If a substitution becomes necessary for justified reasons, LOS CRISTALES TRAVEL AGENCY will endeavour to provide a service of equivalent or superior characteristics, after informing the passenger.
